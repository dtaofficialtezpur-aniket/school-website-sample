About us

A school built on quiet confidence.

Founded in 1998 by a small group of educators, Aurelia began with sixty students and a simple belief: children thrive when they are known, challenged and trusted. Today we are a community of 2,400 learners — still small enough to feel personal.

Our approach blends a strong national curriculum with inquiry-based projects, arts, athletics and service, so every student discovers what they are good at — and who they want to be.

FAQ

Good questions, clear answers.

Which curriculum do you follow?

We follow the national board curriculum enriched with project-based learning, coding from Grade 3 and optional international language tracks.

What are the school timings?

Classes run from 8:00 am to 2:45 pm, Monday to Friday. Co-curricular clubs run until 4:30 pm.

Is transport available?

Yes — a GPS-tracked bus fleet covers most of the city, with trained attendants on every route.

Do you offer scholarships?

Merit and need-based scholarships are available from Grade 6 onwards. Details are shared during the admission process.

What is the class size?

We cap classes at 28 students, with an overall student-to-teacher ratio of 14:1.
